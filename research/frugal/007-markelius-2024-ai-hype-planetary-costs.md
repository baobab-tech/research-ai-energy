# The Mechanisms of AI Hype and Its Planetary and Social Costs

**Topic:** Frugal AI - When NOT to Use AI
**Source:** Markelius, Wright, Kuiper, Delille, Kuo, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-024-00461-2

## Insight

This paper dissects the phenomenon of AI hype, arguing that the current wave is "historically unmatched" in magnitude and planetary costs. AI is being overused and misappropriated across domains where it may not be necessary, driven by corporate "fear of missing out," proliferation of self-proclaimed AI experts, and anthropomorphization of systems. This hype exerts tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption, resulting in environmental costs that often exceed any benefits.

## Key Points

- **Mechanisms driving AI overuse:** Anthropomorphism, FOMO-driven adoption, proliferation of "AI experts," geopolitical competition, and misappropriation of the term "AI" for systems that don't warrant it
- **Planetary costs:** AI hype drives massive resource consumption and energy use for applications where simpler solutions would suffice
- **Social costs:** Perpetuates socio-economic inequalities through redistribution of wealth to AI infrastructure rather than human intelligence
- **Systemic problem:** Overuse is a feature of the hype cycle, not a bug - incentive structures favor AI adoption regardless of appropriateness
- **Recommendation:** Developers, regulators, and the public should navigate the relationship between innovation, investment, and scientific exploration more critically
