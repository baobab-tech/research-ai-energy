# TinyML: Machine Learning for Resource-Constrained Edge Devices

**Topic:** Frugal AI - Efficient Architectures
**Source:** Capogrosso, Cunico, Cheng, Fummi, Cristani, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3365349

## Insight

Tiny Machine Learning (TinyML) promotes the joint design of resource-constrained IoT hardware devices and their learning-based software architectures. This survey demonstrates how AI can be deployed effectively on edge devices with minimal computational resources, enabling applications in smart cities, automotive, and medical robotics while avoiding the energy costs of cloud-based inference.

## Key Data

- Three implementation workflows identified: ML-oriented, HW-oriented, and co-design approaches
- Model optimization techniques include: quantization, pruning, knowledge distillation, and neural architecture search
- Deployment targets: milliwatt-scale microcontrollers with KB of memory
- Energy reduction: Edge inference eliminates data transmission energy and reduces latency
- Application domains: smart cities, automotive, medical devices, industrial IoT
