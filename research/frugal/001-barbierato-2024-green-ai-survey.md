# Toward Green AI: A Methodological Survey of Scientific Literature

**Topic:** Frugal AI - Efficient Architectures
**Source:** Barbierato & Gatti, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3360705

## Insight

This survey contrasts "Red AI" (resource-intensive training on large datasets prioritizing performance) with "Green AI" (efficiency-focused approaches balancing accuracy with environmental impact). The authors identify a fundamental tension in the AI industry: unless complex training procedures mitigate environmental impact, achieving production-level accuracy will conflict with sustainability objectives of major AI vendors.

## Key Data

- Red AI: Models trained using resource-intensive methodologies on very large datasets, causing substantial energy usage and carbon emissions
- Green AI: Achieved through smaller datasets, less computationally intensive training techniques, or sustainable energy resources
- Cluster analysis: Literature organized into (i) Red AI causes/remedies, (ii) environmental impact of Deep Learning deployment, (iii) Green AI techniques for cost mitigation
- Key tension: Decreasing accuracy of production AI systems may be necessary unless training procedures become more sustainable
