# A Survey on Hardware Accelerators for Large Language Models

**Topic:** Efficient AI / Hardware Efficiency Survey
**Source:** Kachris, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/app15020586

## Insight

This comprehensive survey examines the landscape of hardware accelerators designed to enhance the performance and energy efficiency of large language models. As LLMs scale in sophistication, there is a "pressing need to address the computational challenges associated with their scale and complexity." The survey covers GPUs, FPGAs, and custom-designed architectures, providing analysis of architecture, performance metrics, and energy efficiency considerations.

This survey is directly relevant to understanding the hardware options for energy-efficient LLM deployment:

The survey encompasses:
- Architecture design for LLM workloads
- Performance metrics and benchmarks
- **Energy efficiency considerations** (explicit focus)

## Gap/Limitation

As a survey, this paper synthesizes existing work rather than providing new efficiency measurements. However, it provides a useful framework for comparing different hardware approaches to efficient AI.
