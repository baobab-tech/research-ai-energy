# The Effects of Over-Reliance on AI Dialogue Systems on Students' Cognitive Abilities: A Systematic Review

**Topic:** Frugal AI - When NOT to Use AI
**Source:** Zhai, Wibowo, Li, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-024-00316-7

## Insight

Over-reliance on AI dialogue systems (like ChatGPT) is negatively impacting students' cognitive abilities, including critical thinking, decision-making, and analytical reasoning. This systematic review of 14 articles found that individuals increasingly favor "fast and optimal solutions" over thoughtful, slow approaches constrained by practicality. The preference for efficient AI shortcuts occurs even when the AI presents ethical issues or produces errors, representing a fundamental human tendency to take cognitive shortcuts.

## Key Points

- **When AI use becomes counterproductive:** When users accept AI-generated recommendations without question, leading to errors in task performance and decision-making
- **Cognitive cost:** Over-reliance degrades critical thinking, decision-making, and analytical reasoning capabilities
- **Root cause:** Users struggle to assess AI reliability and appropriate trust levels, defaulting to over-trust
- **Educational context warning:** AI dialogue systems may harm learning outcomes when students prioritize speed over understanding
- **Broader implication:** The cognitive shortcut preference (heuristics) explains why users prefer AI even amid recognized ethical issues
