# Strategic Development of Memristors for Neuromorphic Systems: Low-Power and Reconfigurable Operation

**Topic:** Efficient AI / Neuromorphic Hardware (Memristors)
**Source:** Lee, Han, Kang, Hong, Lee & Jeon, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.202413916

## Insight

This review in Advanced Materials examines reconfigurable memristors that integrate both volatile and non-volatile behaviors within a single unit. The paper argues that the "ongoing global energy crisis" has heightened demand for low-power electronic devices, making neuromorphic computing - inspired by the parallel processing and energy efficiency of human brains - increasingly relevant. Memristors address the von Neumann bottleneck that limits conventional computing architectures.

## Key Data

- **31 citations** as of January 2025 (high uptake for recent review)
- Published in **Advanced Materials** (top-tier materials science journal)
- Covers: dual-mode operation, physical mechanisms, structural designs, material properties, switching behaviors, and applications
- Focus on **low-power-consumption solutions** within memristor-based neural networks

## Key Technical Advantages

Reconfigurable memristors offer:
1. **High density** - compact form factor
2. **Low power consumption** - fundamental energy advantage over CMOS
3. **Adaptability** - reconfigurable behavior in single device
4. **In-memory computing** - eliminates data movement energy costs

## Relevance to Frugal AI

This review provides the technical foundation for understanding memristor-based neuromorphic computing as an alternative to GPU-centric AI. The paper "critically evaluates the challenges in deploying reconfigurable memristors as standalone devices or within artificial neural systems" - important for understanding deployment barriers.

## Gap/Limitation

Studies on reconfigurable memristors "remain sparse and are often limited to specific materials such as Mott insulators without fully addressing their unique reconfigurability." This indicates the technology is still maturing.
