## Methodology Notes

- Trace-based simulations with 10 geographically-distributed data centers
- Serving inference requests for large language model
- Compares request direction approaches: Local, Balance, CarbonMin
- First paper to address environmental inequity of AI through geographical load balancing

## Key Insight: Geographic Load Balancing

- Existing GLB approaches may AMPLIFY environmental inequity
- Proposed equity-aware GLB can significantly reduce regional disparity
- Environmental justice concern: AI's footprint disproportionately affects certain regions
- Need to balance efficiency optimization with fairness considerations

## Policy Implications

- Simple carbon/water minimization strategies can worsen inequity
- Regions with cheap/abundant water may absorb disproportionate environmental burden
- Equity-aware approaches needed for responsible AI deployment
- Social-ecological justice concerns require geographic consideration
