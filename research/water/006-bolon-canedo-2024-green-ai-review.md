# Green AI: Strategies for Environmentally Sustainable Machine Learning

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Direct Use
**Source:** Bolon-Canedo, Moran-Fernandez, Cancela, Alonso-Betanzos (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2024.128096

## Insight

This comprehensive review defines "Green AI" as AI that produces accurate results without increasing computational cost - making it both more environmentally friendly and more inclusive (any researcher with a laptop can participate without expensive cloud servers). The paper systematically examines three approaches: "green-by AI" (using AI for eco-friendly practices in other fields), "green-in AI" (designing energy-efficient ML algorithms and models), and tools for measuring and optimizing energy consumption. Critically, reducing computational cost directly reduces both energy and water consumption in data centers.

## Key Data

- Three pillars of Green AI: green-by AI, green-in AI, and measurement/optimization tools
- Goal: Accurate results without increasing computational cost
- Benefit: Democratizes AI research by reducing need for expensive cloud infrastructure
- Regulatory context: Examines role of regulations in promoting green AI
