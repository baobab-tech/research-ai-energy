# CSR and Water Supply: Gaps in Corporate Water Accountability

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Claims Scrutiny
**Source:** Silva Rodriguez de San Miguel (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/su16083183

## Insight

This systematic review examines Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and sustainability practices specifically in water supply contexts. The paper identifies persistent gaps between stated CSR commitments and actual water stewardship outcomes. Key finding: corporate water disclosure often emphasizes efficiency improvements and future targets while underreporting absolute consumption growth and local watershed impacts. This mirrors the pattern in tech company "water positive" pledges.

## Key Claims vs Reality

- **Claim:** CSR frameworks ensure corporate water responsibility
- **Reality:** Water stewardship metrics vary by company; local community impacts often excluded from reporting; efficiency gains may be offset by absolute consumption growth

## Gaps Identified in Water Claims

- Lack of standardized water accounting across industries
- Limited disclosure of watershed-level impacts
- Emphasis on "efficiency" rather than absolute consumption limits
- Minimal third-party verification of water claims
- Disconnect between corporate headquarters reporting and local facility impacts
