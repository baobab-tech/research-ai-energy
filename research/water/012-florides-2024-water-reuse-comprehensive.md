# Water Reuse: A Comprehensive Review

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Reuse/Recycling
**Source:** Florides, Giannakoudi, Ioannou, Lazaridou, Lamprinidou, Loukoutos, Spyridou, Tosounidis, Xanthopoulou & Katsoyiannis (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/environments11040081

## Insight

This comprehensive review examines water reuse as a sustainable solution to global water scarcity, including industrial applications relevant to data center cooling systems. The paper addresses the full lifecycle of water consumption and reuse, examining legislative frameworks, recovery methods, and public perceptions. For data centers, water reuse presents an opportunity to reduce freshwater intake and minimize the environmental impact of evaporative cooling systems.

## Key Data

- Water scarcity now affects all five continents
- Water reuse covers the entire lifecycle from unregulated consumption to various recovery methods
- Legislative frameworks in Europe provide models for industrial water reuse standards
- Public perception remains a barrier to widespread water reuse adoption

## Reuse Technologies Reviewed

- Multiple water recovery methods analyzed for industrial application
- Focus on European regulatory compliance standards
- Addresses both household and industrial-scale reuse applications
- Emphasizes need for standardized quality metrics for recycled water

## Relevance to Data Centers

- Industrial cooling systems can incorporate recycled water sources
- Regulatory frameworks may require water reuse compliance in future
- Public perception less relevant for industrial (vs. potable) reuse applications
- Economic analysis of reuse vs. freshwater intake needed for cooling systems
