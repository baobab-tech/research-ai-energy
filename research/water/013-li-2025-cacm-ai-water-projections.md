# AI Global Water Demand: 4.2-6.6 Billion Cubic Meters by 2027

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Numbers Compilation
**Source:** Li, Yang, Islam, Ren (2025)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3724499

## Key Numbers

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| GPT-3 training | 700,000 liters | Direct water evaporated (Microsoft US data centers) |
| ChatGPT inference | 500 mL | Per 20-50 queries (varies by location/season) |
| ChatGPT per query | 10-25 mL | Implied range per single query |
| Global AI demand 2027 | 4.2-6.6 billion m3 | Projected water withdrawal |
| Comparison: Denmark | 4-6 countries | Equivalent annual water withdrawal |
| Comparison: UK | 0.5 UK | Equivalent annual water withdrawal |

## Methodology Notes

- Published in Communications of the ACM (peer-reviewed, upgraded from 2023 arXiv preprint)
- Methodology based on Microsoft's disclosed Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) and Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE)
- Strong spatial-temporal variability noted: cooling needs differ dramatically by location and season
- Primary driver of direct consumption: evaporative cooling systems

## Regional Variations

- West Des Moines data centers showed higher water consumption than other locations
- Summer months significantly increase water usage for cooling
- On-site vs off-site water consumption varies based on cooling system design
- Authors call for transparency from AI companies about water usage

## Manufacturing Context

- Training GPT-3 water consumption equivalent to manufacturing 370 BMW cars
- Or equivalent to manufacturing 320 Tesla electric vehicles
- Highlights embodied water costs beyond direct operational consumption
