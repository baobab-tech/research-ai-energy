# Google 2025: Water Consumption Up 34% to 10,869 Million Gallons; Replenishment Covers 78% of Freshwater Consumed, and the Denominator Excludes Reclaimed Water

**Topic:** Water — corporate disclosure FY2025; withdrawal vs consumption; replenishment pledge accounting; provenance of the 0.26 mL per-prompt figure
**Source:** Google/Alphabet, 2026 Environmental Report (calendar year 2025)
**Type:** corporate disclosure
**URL:** https://sustainability.google/files/1699a2e19277e478b38a659f00cb38398a836e8c/download-1699a2e1.pdf
**Published:** 2026 (data for 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2025)

## Finding

Google withdrew 14,689 million gallons and consumed 10,869 million gallons in 2025, a 34% rise in consumption year over year; data centres account for 97% of the consumption. Its 2030 target is to replenish 120% of the **freshwater** it consumes, and the 2025 result is 78%. The denominator excludes reclaimed wastewater and seawater, so the 7,717 Mgal replenished is measured against 9,947 Mgal of freshwater consumption rather than the 10,869 Mgal actually consumed — 71% on the total-consumption basis. Google's per-prompt water figure is reprinted unchanged at 0.26 mL, and the report's own footnote states it was derived by applying Google's **2024 average fleetwide water usage effectiveness** to a May 2025 energy-per-prompt measurement. The 2026 report publishes no fleetwide WUE value anywhere; the string "WUE" does not appear in it.

## Methodology

Consumption = withdrawal − discharge; it is a residual, not a measured quantity. Withdrawal is metered or invoiced where available; at data centres lacking data, Google estimates from partial-year data or withdrawal-to-discharge ratios. Discharge, where unmetered, uses a 90% discharge flow factor on domestic withdrawal and 0% on irrigation withdrawal, or "engineering principles" at remaining data centres. Seawater and brackish water (TDS > 1,000 mg/L) are excluded from all reported metrics.

Replenishment is quantified by LimnoTech under Volumetric Water Benefit Accounting 2.0, counting only projects active within watersheds Google's operations rely on and with confirmed volumetric benefits in the reporting year. 2025 metrics were subject to third-party limited assurance.

The 0.26 mL per-prompt figure (footnote 50): a point-in-time analysis of energy per median Gemini App text-generation prompt using May 2025 data, with water estimated from that energy by applying Google's 2024 average fleetwide WUE. Google states it does not represent all prompts and is not indicative of future performance.

## Limitations and conflicts

Self-reported by the company whose pledge is at issue; limited assurance only (a review, not an audit), and the 2030 ambition is not assured.

- **Consumption is derived, not metered.** With discharge partly estimated by a flat 90% factor, consumption inherits that error at every site without discharge metering.
- **The replenishment denominator is chosen, not given.** Excluding reclaimed wastewater from freshwater consumption removes 922 Mgal (8.5%) from the target basis in 2025. Douglas County GA alone withdraws 394.3 Mgal of reclaimed water. Defensible on the grounds that reclaimed water is not a freshwater draw — but it means "replenish more water than we consume" and "replenish 120% of freshwater consumed" are different commitments, and the report uses the first phrasing in its ambition statement and the second in its target.
- **Watershed matching is asserted at portfolio level.** 165 projects across 97 watersheds against roughly 30 reported data-centre locations. Google states benefits are counted only from watersheds its operations rely on, but publishes no site-by-watershed reconciliation, so whether replenishment lands in the basins under most stress cannot be checked from this document.
- **No fleetwide WUE is published**, while the per-prompt water figure depends on one. The 1.15 L/kWh used in the Gemini technical paper cannot be verified against this report.
- **No off-site water.** Nothing here covers the water consumed generating Google's electricity.
- Growth outruns efficiency: replenishment rose from 4,590 to 7,717 Mgal (+68%) while freshwater consumption rose 37%, so the ratio improved from 63% to 78% — but the 2030 portfolio capacity of 19,722 Mgal is only 198% of 2025 freshwater consumption, and consumption is compounding at over 30% a year.
