# Comparison of Cooling Tower Blowdown and Enhanced Make Up Water Treatment

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Reuse/Recycling
**Source:** Muller, Chapanova, Diekow, Kaiser, Hamelink, Hitsov, Wyseure, Moed, Palmowski & Wintgens (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2024.121949

## Insight

This study directly addresses cooling tower water reuse strategies, comparing two approaches to minimize water footprint in recirculating evaporative cooling systems: (1) reusing cooling tower blowdown water, and (2) producing demineralized water to increase cycles of concentration. The techno-economic analysis found that blowdown reuse achieves 13% water savings with lower implementation and operation costs than enhanced make-up water treatment. This is directly applicable to data center evaporative cooling systems.

## Key Data

- **Water savings:** 13% through blowdown reuse (option 1)
- **Applicable when:** Cycles of concentration > 3; blowdown conductivity ~2 mS/cm; TOC ~20 mg/L
- **Treatment achieved:** Product water 80 uS/cm conductivity; 60 ug/L TOC
- **Enhanced make-up treatment:** 20 uS/cm conductivity; 70 ug/L TOC

## Reuse Technologies

- Biologically activated carbon filtration
- Ultrafiltration
- Reverse osmosis
- Combined treatment train produces high-quality permeate

## Economic Analysis

- Blowdown reuse: lower implementation costs vs. enhanced make-up
- Blowdown reuse: lower operation costs vs. enhanced make-up
- Pilot-scale trials validated feasibility of both approaches
- Applicable to industrial cooling systems operating at high cycles of concentration

## Relevance to Data Centers

- Evaporative cooling towers are common in large data centers
- Blowdown reuse directly applicable to data center water management
- 13% water savings significant for facilities consuming millions of gallons annually
- Treatment train (filtration + UF + RO) can be integrated into data center water systems
