# Evaluation of Return Cooling Water Reuse in Wet Cooled Power Plant

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Reuse/Recycling
**Source:** Biedunkova, Kuznietsov & Korbutiak (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scenv.2024.100151

## Insight

This study demonstrates successful cooling water reuse in an operating wet-cooled power plant, achieving up to 25% blending of treated cooling tower blowdown (CTBD) with make-up water without requiring increased chemical dosing. The research validates a lime softening treatment approach for recycling cooling water while maintaining water quality standards. This provides a practical model for data centers using evaporative cooling systems to reduce freshwater consumption.

## Key Data

- **Reuse rate:** Up to 25% CTBD blended with make-up water
- **Treatment:** Lime softening (CaO) without increased dose requirements
- **Total hardness maintained:** 2.2 mg-eq/dm3
- **Chloride concentration:** 200 mg/dm3 (within regulatory standards)
- **Sulfate concentration:** 250 mg/dm3 (within regulatory standards)
- **Discharge velocity:** 0.7 m/s maintained within regulatory standards

## Treatment Approach

- Lime softening treatment for CTBD
- Laboratory modeling validated treatment effectiveness
- No increased CaO dose required for 25% blending ratio
- Recycled water used directly as cooling tower supply

## Environmental Sustainability Benefits

- Reduces freshwater intake by up to 25%
- Minimizes wastewater discharge to environment
- Maintains regulatory compliance for water quality
- Demonstrates practical circular water economy for cooling systems

## Relevance to Data Centers

- Wet-cooled power plants analogous to data center evaporative cooling systems
- 25% water savings applicable to large-scale cooling operations
- Lime softening is established, cost-effective treatment technology
- Regulatory compliance framework provides model for data center water management
