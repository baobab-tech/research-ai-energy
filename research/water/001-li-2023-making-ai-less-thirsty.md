# GPT-3 Training Evaporates 700,000 Liters of Water

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Direct Use
**Source:** Li, Yang, Islam, Ren (2023)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2304.03271

## Insight

This seminal paper is the first to systematically quantify AI's "secret water footprint." The authors estimate that training GPT-3 in Microsoft's US data centers directly evaporated approximately 700,000 liters of clean freshwater - enough to produce 370 BMW cars or 320 Tesla electric vehicles. More alarmingly, they project global AI demand will account for 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters of water withdrawal by 2027, exceeding the total annual water withdrawal of 4-6 Denmark-sized countries or half of the United Kingdom.

## Key Data

- Training GPT-3: ~700,000 liters direct water consumption (Microsoft US data centers)
- Inference: ChatGPT "drinks" 500ml of water per 20-50 queries (varies by location/season)
- Global AI water demand by 2027: 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters projected
- Comparison: Equivalent to annual water withdrawal of 4-6 Denmarks or 0.5 UK

## Notes

- Methodology: Modeled based on Microsoft's disclosed Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) and Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE) metrics
- Key insight: Water footprint has strong spatial-temporal variability - cooling needs differ dramatically by location and season
- Data center cooling type: Evaporative cooling is primary driver of direct consumption
- The paper calls for transparency from AI companies about water usage
- Authors from UC Riverside and UT Arlington - academic research, no obvious industry conflict
