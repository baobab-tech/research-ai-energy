# ESG Reporting Gaps: Why Water Claims Need Scrutiny

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Claims Scrutiny
**Source:** Chopra, Senadheera, Dissanayake, Withana, Chib, Rhee, Ok (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/su16020606

## Insight

This comprehensive review of ESG reporting challenges identifies fundamental problems with corporate environmental disclosure: lack of standardization, inconsistent metrics, limited third-party verification, and regulatory gaps. These systemic issues mean that "water positive" claims from tech companies operate in a disclosure environment where self-reported data goes largely unverified. The authors emphasize the path toward broader sustainable development requires addressing these reporting deficiencies.

## Key Claims vs Reality

- **Claim:** ESG reports provide transparent environmental accountability
- **Reality:** Reporting frameworks vary widely; verification requirements are minimal; companies have discretion in what to disclose and how to measure it

## Implications for Water Claims

- Tech companies self-report water consumption and replenishment metrics
- No standardized methodology for measuring "water positive" status
- Different boundaries (operational vs supply chain) affect reported figures
- Watershed-specific impacts not captured in aggregate water accounting
