# AI Hype Exerts "Tremendous Pressure" on Finite Resources

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Direct Use
**Source:** Markelius, Wright, Kuiper, Delille, Kuo (2024)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-024-00461-2

## Insight

This paper analyzes the mechanisms driving AI hype and argues the current AI wave is "historically unmatched in terms of magnitude, scale and planetary and social costs." The authors identify that AI hype "exerts tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption" as the "fear of missing out" drives rushed adoption. This systemic analysis helps explain WHY water (and energy) consumption is growing so rapidly - it's not just technical necessity but market dynamics and hype cycles pushing unsustainable expansion.

## Key Data

- Assessment: Current AI hype wave is historically unprecedented in planetary costs
- Mechanisms identified: Anthropomorphism, self-proclaimed AI "experts", geopolitical competition, private sector FOMO
- Consequence: Tremendous pressure on finite resources including water

## Notes

- Perspective: Critical AI ethics/policy analysis
- Key contribution: Explains systemic drivers behind resource consumption growth
- Links AI expansion to socio-economic injustices and wealth redistribution
- Offers recommendations for developers, regulators, and public to mitigate hype
- Academic research focused on governance implications
- Relevant for understanding WHY water consumption keeps growing despite awareness
