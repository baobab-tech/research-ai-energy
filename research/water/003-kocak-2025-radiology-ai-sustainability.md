# AI in Healthcare: The Sustainability Paradox

**Topic:** Water Consumption - Direct Use
**Source:** Kocak, Ponsiglione, Romeo, Ugga, Huisman, Cuocolo (2025)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s13244-025-01962-2

## Insight

This medical imaging review explicitly addresses AI's environmental footprint including water consumption as part of a broader "sustainability paradox" - AI promises efficiency gains but comes with significant environmental costs. The paper argues that radiology departments adopting AI must consider the full environmental picture: energy consumption, carbon emissions, hardware waste, AND water use from data storage and cloud computing. This represents a growing recognition in specialized fields that AI adoption decisions should factor in environmental sustainability.

## Key Data

- AI systems (especially deep learning): require substantial computational resources
- Cloud computing and data storage: identified as exacerbating environmental impact
- Three dimensions of concern: environmental, economic, and social sustainability
- Healthcare AI identified as having "ambivalent potential" - can both help and harm sustainability

## Notes

- Perspective: Healthcare/radiology sector examining AI sustainability
- Key insight: Even beneficial AI applications carry hidden environmental costs that must be weighed
- Calls for individual, institutional, and collective-level strategies to maximize benefits while minimizing impacts
- Authors are European radiologists/researchers - medical perspective on AI environmental costs
- Part of growing literature recognizing water/energy costs across all AI application domains
