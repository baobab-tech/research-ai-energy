# Understanding Environmental Impact of Generative AI Services

**Topic:** Water Consumption - GenAI Infrastructure Analysis
**Source:** Berthelot, Caron, Jay, Lefevre (2025)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3725984

## Insight

This 2025 Communications of the ACM paper provides detailed analysis of environmental impacts from generative AI service infrastructure, focusing on the operational/inference phase rather than training. The authors from INRIA France examine the full-stack environmental footprint of GenAI services, including cooling systems that drive water consumption. This complements Li et al.'s water-specific analysis with broader environmental accounting.

## Key Contributions

| Aspect | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Inference vs Training | Inference now dominates operational environmental impact as GenAI scales |
| Infrastructure scope | Full-stack analysis includes cooling, not just compute power |
| Regional variation | Environmental impact varies significantly by data center location |
| Measurement gaps | Identifies what companies should but don't disclose |

## Relevance to Water Research

The paper addresses the shift from training-focused to inference-focused environmental analysis. For water consumption:

- Training happens once; inference runs continuously at scale
- Cooling requirements for inference clusters are substantial
- Water footprint of serving billions of daily queries exceeds training
- Authors advocate for standardized measurement and disclosure
