## Prices: the mechanism and the counter-evidence

Both directions are represented because the evidence runs both ways.

**Attribution by the statutory monitor.** Monitoring Analytics re-cleared PJM's 2027/28 capacity
auction with data-centre load removed from the forecast. Revenues fall from $16.41bn to $9.91bn.
Data centres account for $6.50bn (+65.5%) in that year and $23.10bn across three auctions. Of the
17,071 MW driving the result, 13,018 MW does not yet exist
([016](016-monitoring-analytics-2026-pjm-capacity-data-center.md)). The 2028/29 auction cleared at
the $325/MW-day cap and 6,831 MW short of the reliability requirement, with 524.7 MW of genuinely
new generation ([017](017-pjm-2026-bra-2028-2029-shortfall.md)).

**The opposite result.** An instrumental-variable study finds a 10% rise in data-centre capacity
lowered residential rates about 0.4% over 2015 to 2024, on the mechanism that retail tariffs
recover average cost and durable new load spreads embedded fixed costs
([018](018-watten-2026-data-centers-lowered-rates-iv.md)). Two of three authors work for EPRI and
the first-stage F statistic is 7.8, a weak instrument. The authors' own caveats, gas turbine
backlogs and load that fails to materialise, are the conditions that reverse the sign, and those
conditions now hold. The two studies agree on the economics and differ on whether the system is
supply-constrained.

## Cost allocation

Pennsylvania's PUC assigns network upgrade costs to loads above 50 MW on a "but-for" test, with
13 of 14 utilities participating ([019](019-pa-puc-2026-large-load-but-for-tariff.md)).
Large-load tariffs grew from 41 to 104 in a year, typically at ~50 MW thresholds with 10 to 20
year terms, about 80% minimum take, and collateral requirements
([020](020-sepa-nccetc-2026-delta-large-load-tariffs.md)).

## Environmental justice

Damage is predominantly Scope 2 and lands away from host communities. Modelled US data-centre
health costs reach $20.9bn and 1,262 deaths in 2028 under high growth, with over 90% arising at
the generating plants supplying them. The worst-affected county bears about 7x the
national per-household burden and about 200x the lowest, correlating at r=0.842 with existing
fuel-combustion burden. Loudoun County, the densest US cluster, ranks tenth
([023](023-han-2026-data-center-health-costs-county.md)).

On-site generation is the exception. xAI ran 33 unpermitted gas turbines near Memphis, up from 27
after the notice of intent to sue, with a potential to emit 2,508 tons of NOx a year into an
ozone non-attainment airshed ([022](022-naacp-xai-2026-memphis-turbines-clean-air-act.md)).
Nationally, 74 gas plants are proposed for data centres totalling 143 GW and 662 Mt CO2e a year,
88% of them in counties with below-average income
([024](024-environmental-integrity-2026-gas-plants-for-data-centers.md)).

Coal retirements fell to 2.6 GW in 2025, the lowest since 2010, with utility plans showing 4 GW
less coal retirement and 8 GW more gas ([025](025-eia-rmi-2026-retirement-delays-data-center-load.md)).

## Claims that do not hold up

**"Cancellations quadrupled in 2025" is unsupported.** It traces to an unpublished Baird equity
research note with no title, date, methodology or definition of "cancellation" in any secondary
coverage. The main tracker reports 833 opposition groups by March 2026, but it has no
denominator, is run by a vendor to AI companies, and jumped from 396 to 833 in one quarter, which
is consistent with improved detection ([021](021-data-center-watch-2026-opposition-tracking.md)).

**Property-value effects are unevidenced in either direction.** Both 2026 analyses are null,
small (n=80 in one), non-peer-reviewed, and were presented publicly by a developer
([027](027-property-values-2026-null-findings-thin-evidence.md)).

## Gaps

- Water cost-shifting cannot be verified. About 97% of on-site water comes from municipal
  systems, and rates and service agreements are largely not public, with some agreements barring
  disclosure ([026](026-crs-2026-data-centers-water-municipal-supply.md)).
- No census-tract-level peer-reviewed siting study against race and income.
- Oregon PUC Order 26-154 (UM 2377), the first POWER Act implementation, is unretrieved.
- Pennsylvania's numeric tariff terms are in the order itself, not the press release.
