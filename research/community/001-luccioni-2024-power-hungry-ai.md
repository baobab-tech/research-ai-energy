# Power Hungry AI: Energy Costs of Generative Systems

**Topic:** Community Impact - Energy Burden
**Source:** Luccioni, Jernite, Strubell, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3630106.3658542

## Insight

Generative, multi-purpose AI systems consume orders of magnitude more energy than task-specific models. This study provides the first systematic comparison of inference energy costs across ML systems, finding that the "generality" promised by large language models comes at a steep environmental cost. This energy burden ultimately affects communities through electricity infrastructure strain and grid reliability concerns.

## Key Details

- Finding: Multi-purpose generative AI architectures are orders of magnitude more expensive than task-specific systems
- Measurement: Energy and carbon required to perform 1,000 inferences on benchmark datasets
- Implication: Commercial AI deployment decisions should weigh utility against increased energy and emissions costs
- Context: Recent surge in commercial AI products based on generative systems drives infrastructure demand
