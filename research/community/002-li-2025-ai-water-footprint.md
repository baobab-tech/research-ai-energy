# Making AI Less 'Thirsty': Hidden Water Costs of AI Models

**Topic:** Community Impact - Water Scarcity
**Source:** Li, Yang, Islam, Ren, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3724499

## Insight

AI data centers have a "secret water footprint" that is often unrecognized by the public and policymakers. This paper uncovers the hidden water consumption of AI models, particularly in data center cooling systems. For communities in water-stressed regions, data center expansion directly competes with residential and agricultural water needs.

## Key Details

- Published in: Communications of the ACM (2025)
- Focus: Uncovering hidden water footprint of AI models
- Relevance: Data center cooling systems are major water consumers
- Implication: Water scarcity concerns drive community opposition to data center siting
