# AI and Sustainability: Balancing Promise and Impact

**Topic:** Community Impact - Sustainability Concerns
**Source:** Dhiman, Miteff, Wang, Ma, Amirikas, Fabian, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/analytics3010008

## Insight

Despite enthusiasm surrounding AI's potential, concerns persist about substantial energy consumption and ethical challenges. This comprehensive review categorizes literature into "Sustainability of AI" (environmental costs of AI systems) and "AI for Sustainability" (using AI to address environmental problems), revealing tension between AI's promise and its actual impacts. The paper identifies a notable trend toward holistic approaches but notes the economic dimension remains under-explored.
