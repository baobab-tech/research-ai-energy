# The Mechanisms of AI Hype and Its Planetary and Social Costs

**Topic:** Community Impact - Environmental Justice
**Source:** Markelius, Wright, Kuiper, Delille, Kuo, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-024-00461-2

## Insight

AI hype is historically unmatched in magnitude, scale, and planetary/social costs. The paper identifies mechanisms fueling this hype including anthropomorphism, proliferation of self-proclaimed "experts," geopolitical and private sector "fear of missing out," and misappropriation of the term "AI." Current AI development exerts "tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption" while perpetuating socio-economic inequalities through redistribution of wealth away from human intelligence.

## Key Details

- Published in: AI and Ethics (2024)
- Framework: Examines connection between AI hype and socio-economic injustices
- Finding: Current wave of AI hype is historically unmatched in terms of costs
- Mechanisms identified: Anthropomorphism, FOMO, geopolitical competition, term misuse
- Community relevance: Huge redistribution of costs to local communities while benefits flow to tech companies
