# The Mechanisms of AI Hype and Its Planetary and Social Costs

**Topic:** Narrative - AI for Climate Claims
**Source:** Markelius, Wright, Kuiper, Delille, Kuo, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-024-00461-2

## Insight

This paper dissects AI hype as a phenomenon with significant large-scale consequences. The authors conclude that current AI hype is "historically unmatched in terms of magnitude, scale and planetary and social costs." The paper identifies socio-technical mechanisms fueling the hype: anthropomorphism, proliferation of self-proclaimed "experts," geopolitical and private sector "fear of missing out," and overuse/misappropriation of the term "AI" in emerging technologies. Crucially, AI hype exerts tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption while perpetuating socio-economic inequalities.

## Claims vs Evidence

- **Claim:** AI represents transformative climate solutions
- **Evidence:** AI hype creates a "huge associated redistribution of wealth and costs to human intelligence" while exerting pressure on resources and energy

- **Claim:** AI benefits justify resource consumption
- **Evidence:** Current hype is historically unmatched in planetary and social costs, including perpetuation of social inequalities
