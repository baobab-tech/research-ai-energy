# Getting Beyond Net Zero Dashboards in IT Sector

**Topic:** Narrative - AI for Climate Claims
**Source:** Gregg, Strengers, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.erss.2023.103397

## Insight

This paper from Energy Research & Social Science critiques how the information technology sector approaches Net Zero commitments. The authors argue that current corporate sustainability dashboards and metrics create a false sense of progress while failing to address fundamental tensions between growth-oriented business models and genuine emissions reductions. The IT sector's Net Zero claims often rely on accounting mechanisms (renewable energy certificates, carbon offsets) that may not represent actual decarbonization.

## Claims vs Evidence

- **Claim:** Tech companies are achieving Net Zero through dashboards and metrics
- **Evidence:** Current approaches create illusion of progress while avoiding structural changes to business models

- **Claim:** Sustainability metrics demonstrate climate leadership
- **Evidence:** Dashboard approaches may obscure rather than illuminate actual environmental impact
