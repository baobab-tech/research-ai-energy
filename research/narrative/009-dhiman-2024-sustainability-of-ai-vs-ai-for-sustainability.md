# Sustainability OF AI vs AI FOR Sustainability: The Critical Distinction

**Topic:** Narrative - Net Impact Analysis
**Source:** Dhiman, Miteff, Wang, Ma, Amirikas & Fabian, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/analytics3010008

## Insight

This review paper introduces a critical framework for analyzing AI's net environmental impact by systematically categorizing literature into two streams: "Sustainability OF AI" (reducing AI's own environmental footprint) and "AI FOR Sustainability" (using AI to address environmental challenges). The authors identify a notable trend toward holistic approaches that consider both dimensions, with a surge in publications since 2019 signaling the field's maturity. They reveal that the economic dimension remains under-explored, and call for better alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## Benefits vs Costs

| Benefits | Costs |
|----------|-------|
| AI can optimize energy systems | AI has substantial energy consumption |
| AI can improve resource efficiency | Training large models emits significant CO2 |
| AI enables precision agriculture | Hardware manufacturing has environmental costs |
| AI accelerates climate modeling | Ethical challenges around data and deployment |

## Net Assessment

- **Is AI net positive or negative for environment?** Paper presents "balanced perspective between the two" - net impact depends on how AI is developed and deployed. Neither inherently positive nor negative.
- **What's missing from the analysis?** Paper identifies gaps in: (1) Economic dimension analysis (2) Stakeholder influence assessment (3) Empirical validation of theoretical benefits (4) Integration with SDG targets (5) Long-term lifecycle impacts

## Framework Contribution

This paper provides essential vocabulary for net impact discussions:
- **Sustainability OF AI**: Making AI itself sustainable (efficient algorithms, green data centers, hardware lifecycle)
- **AI FOR Sustainability**: Deploying AI to solve environmental problems (climate modeling, grid optimization, precision agriculture)

The NET impact = (Benefits from AI FOR Sustainability) - (Costs from Sustainability OF AI)

Most industry claims focus only on "AI FOR" while ignoring "OF AI" costs.
