# 55 Green AI Initiatives Identified - But Most Lack Empirical Validation

**Topic:** Narrative - Net Impact Analysis
**Source:** Alzoubi & Mishra, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2024.143090

## Insight

This comprehensive review identified 55 green AI initiatives across industry and academia, categorized into six themes: cloud optimization, model efficiency, carbon footprinting, sustainability-focused AI development, open-source initiatives, and research community efforts. The critical finding: while these initiatives are widely promoted, research discussing their actual environmental impact "remains scarce." Most tools are in early development stages and have not been empirically validated. The paper warns that "comprehensive analysis are still required to empirically evaluate the majority of other tools."

## Benefits vs Costs

| Benefits | Costs |

| Open-source community engagement | Gap between theoretical potential and actual impact |

## Net Assessment

- **Is AI net positive or negative for environment?** Cannot determine - the tools designed to make AI green lack the empirical evidence to confirm they work. We are flying blind on whether green AI initiatives deliver promised benefits.
- **What's missing from the analysis?** (1) Actual emissions reductions achieved (not just projected) (2) Comparative effectiveness between initiatives (3) Lifecycle assessments (4) Rebound effects when AI becomes more efficient (5) Integration challenges in production environments

## Initiatives Examined Include

- Cloud optimization tools (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud sustainability features)
- Model efficiency techniques (pruning, quantization, distillation)
- Carbon calculators (CodeCarbon, ML CO2 Impact)
- Sustainability-focused development frameworks
- Research community standards

**Key Quote:** "While some tools have been recognized and studied, comprehensive analysis are still required to empirically evaluate the majority of other tools due to their early stages of development in this field."
