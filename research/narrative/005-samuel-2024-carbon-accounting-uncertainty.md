# Carbon Accounting in Digital Industry: Decision Making Under Uncertainty

**Topic:** Narrative - AI for Climate Claims
**Source:** Samuel, Lucivero, Knowles, Wright, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/su16052017

## Insight

Based on qualitative interviews, this study reveals how stakeholders struggle to quantify carbon emissions across complex digital systems. The lack of standardization and difficulty measuring emissions leads stakeholders to call for "more data" - but the authors argue this obscures the fundamental reality that uncertainty will always exist. Rather than waiting for perfect measurement, the digital industry must learn to make decisions and govern emissions within uncertainty. This paper exposes how calls for "better data" can become a mechanism to delay action on AI and digital sector emissions.

## Claims vs Evidence

- **Claim:** More data and better measurement will solve carbon accounting problems
- **Evidence:** Stakeholders struggle with complex systems; there will always be uncertainty, and calls for more data can delay action

- **Claim:** Digital sector can accurately report its carbon footprint
- **Evidence:** Researchers, industry, NGOs, and policy representatives all struggled to quantify and measure carbon across complex systems
