# AI and Sustainability: Beyond the "Honeymoon" to Environmental Justice

**Topic:** Narrative - AI for Climate Claims
**Source:** Moyano-Fernandez, Rueda, Delgado, Ausin, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/11287462.2024.2322208

## Insight

This paper argues that while AI in healthcare has benefits, its environmental impact creates social inequalities and long-term environmental damages that are often overlooked. The authors call for moving beyond a "short and fleeting green honeymoon between health and sustainability caused by AI" toward a "lasting marriage." They advocate for considering AI's environmental impacts from a One Health paradigm that integrates human, animal, and environmental health perspectives. The paper proposes calling for environmental cost transparency and increased "green responsibility" in AI development.

## Claims vs Evidence

- **Claim:** AI in healthcare provides net benefits
- **Evidence:** While AI has benefits, it can produce social inequalities and long-term environmental damages not thoroughly contemplated

- **Claim:** AI represents a sustainable solution
- **Evidence:** Current AI-health relationship is like a "short honeymoon" - health justice should be rethought from an environmental perspective going beyond anthropocentric cost-benefit analysis
