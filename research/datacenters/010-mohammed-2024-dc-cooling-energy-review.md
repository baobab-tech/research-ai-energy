# Data Center Cooling: Critical Review of Energy-Saving Techniques

**Topic:** Data Centers - Growth
**Source:** Mohammed, Salem, Abdelrehim & Ahmed, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrefrig.2024.02.007

## Insight

This International Journal of Refrigeration review documents that data centers are "energy-intensive facilities" with over 95% of their total cooling load attributed to heat generated by IT equipment. As data center infrastructure expands globally to meet AI and cloud computing demands, cooling becomes a critical bottleneck. The paper reviews various energy-saving techniques employed to enhance efficiency and reduce Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE), with particular focus on economizers using outdoor air as the most effective strategy for addressing year-round cooling demands. The analysis highlights that despite the simplicity of cooling systems, analyzing them involves complex dynamic considerations including weather conditions, system operation, and economizer control.

## Key Data

- 95%+ of data center cooling load from IT equipment heat generation
- Economizers using outdoor air: most effective year-round cooling solution
- PUE reduction is primary efficiency metric for data centers
- Dynamic analysis required: weather conditions, system operation, controls
- Various energy-saving techniques reviewed for efficiency enhancement
- Cooling system design critical for data center sustainability
