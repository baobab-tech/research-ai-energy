# Off-Grid Hyperscale Data Centers: Wind and Solar Feasibility

**Topic:** Data Centers - Renewable Power Sources
**Source:** Rollinson, Urquhart & Thomson, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/en18020382

## Insight

This paper directly addresses the tension between data center power demand growth and grid infrastructure constraints that have companies like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft seeking alternatives to traditional grid connections via PPAs. The authors model stand-alone hybrid renewable/gas microgrids for hyperscale data centers across Europe's five major data center hotspots (Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Dublin). Key finding: off-grid hybrid renewable generation can provide BOTH lower cost and lower carbon emissions than grid-equivalent systems for each location. The paper introduces a new metric (LCOEu - Levelised Cost of Energy utilised) to account for the economic implications of generation-consumption mismatch.

## Key Data

- Locations analyzed: Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Dublin (Europe's DC hotspots)
- Hybrid configuration (wind + solar + battery + gas backup) beats grid equivalent on:
  - Levelized cost of energy (LCOE)
  - CO2eq emissions
- LCOEu across all sites: 70-102 GBP/MWh
- Emissions range: 0.021-0.074 tCO2eq/MWh
- Grid infrastructure investment globally has been insufficient
- Both renewable developers and data center operators face grid connection challenges

## Notes

- Published January 2025
- 2 citations (newly published)

- Supports viability of direct renewable power vs PPA accounting claims
