# EU Data Center Sustainability Policy: Planet Proof Computing by 2030

**Topic:** Data Centers - Growth
**Source:** Commins & Irion, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.71265/c1nnwh92

## Insight

This policy paper examines the European Union's regulatory approach to data center sustainability as AI drives "ever-growing ecological footprint" in the digital sector. The EU aims to achieve climate-neutral, energy-efficient and sustainable data centers by 2030 through regulations targeting energy efficiency, equipment recycling, and mandatory reporting/transparency obligations. The paper notes that in 2025, the European Commission will present a report based on information reported by data center operators to review its policy. The authors argue that further regulation should translate reporting requirements into binding sustainability targets and address "rebound effects" - where efficiency gains are offset by increased usage.

## Key Data

- EU target: climate-neutral data centers by 2030
- Policy focus: energy efficiency, equipment recycling, transparency reporting
- 2025: EU Commission report on data center operator submissions
- Rebound effects identified as key concern for policy
- Reporting requirements proposed to become binding sustainability targets
- Public value orientation of data center industry emphasized
