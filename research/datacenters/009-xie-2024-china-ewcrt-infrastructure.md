# China's East-West Computing Resources Transmission Project: 2125 Mt CO2 Reduction Potential

**Topic:** Data Centers - Growth
**Source:** Xie, Han & Tan, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-024-02963-0

## Insight

This Humanities and Social Sciences Communications paper documents China's ambitious East-West Computing Resources Transmission (EWCRT) Project - a massive infrastructure initiative building eight data center hubs and 10 data center clusters across the country. The authors estimate that the project could reduce CO2 emissions by 2,125 metric tons (Mt) between 2020-2050, and when combined with PUE improvements and increased green electricity use, the reduction could reach 9,500 Mt over three decades. This represents a unique approach to data center growth: relocating compute capacity to western regions with abundant renewable energy while transmitting results to eastern population centers.

## Key Data

- 8 data center hubs + 10 data center clusters being constructed
- Estimated CO2 reduction: 2,125 Mt (2020-2050) from relocation alone
- With PUE + green electricity improvements: up to 9,500 Mt CO2 reduction
- Geographic strategy: move computing west to renewable-rich regions
- Using fsQCA (fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis) for configuration analysis
- Goal: transform data centers into "green engines" for digital economy
