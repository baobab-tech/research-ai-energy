# LLM Infrastructure Growth: Life-Cycle Carbon Footprint Warning

**Topic:** Data Centers - Growth
**Source:** Jiang, Sonne, Li, You & You, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eng.2024.04.002

## Insight

Published in Engineering (Elsevier), this paper warns about the "immense increase" in life-cycle energy and carbon footprints as LLM-powered chatbots proliferate globally. The authors identify eight main phases throughout the life cycle of intelligent chatbots that contribute to environmental impact: training, fine-tuning, updating, hardware manufacturing (including GPUs and supercomputers), data/operations management, material recycling, and supporting services. The paper emphasizes that global frontier technology companies are "feverishly" participating in chatbot development, creating unprecedented infrastructure expansion that carries substantial carbon emissions across the entire value chain.

## Key Data

- Eight main lifecycle phases identified for LLM carbon footprints
- Hardware manufacturing (GPUs, supercomputers) significant emissions source
- Training, fine-tuning, and updating all consume substantial electricity
- Material recycling and data management add to lifecycle impacts
- Global frontier companies accelerating chatbot development
- Authors propose three strategic pathways to mitigate footprints
