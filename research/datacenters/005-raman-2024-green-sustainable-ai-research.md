# Green AI Research Identifies Three Major Themes But Gaps Remain

**Topic:** Data Centers - Power Sources
**Source:** Raman, Pattnaik, Lathabai, Kumar, Govindan & Nedungadi, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-024-00920-x

## Insight

This integrated thematic and BERTopic modeling analysis of Green AI and Sustainable AI literature identifies three major research clusters: (1) Responsible AI for Sustainable Development (integrating sustainability and ethics), (2) Advancements in Green AI for Energy Optimization, and (3) Big Data-Driven Computational Advances (socio-economic and environmental aspects). The study reveals a trend toward embedding ethical and sustainability considerations into AI research, with emerging topics including Ethical Healthcare AI Intelligence and Cognitive AI Innovation. However, the analysis shows AI's influence on socio-economic and environmental aspects remains insufficiently studied compared to pure performance optimization.

## Key Data

- Three major thematic clusters identified in Green/Sustainable AI literature
- Five emerging BERTopic themes: Ethical Eco-Intelligence, Sustainable Neural Computing, Ethical Healthcare AI, AI Learning Quest, Cognitive AI Innovation
- Research trending toward embedding sustainability into AI development lifecycle
- Gap: socio-economic and environmental impacts under-researched vs. performance
- Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals emphasized

## Notes

- Methodology: Dual-analytical approach combining thematic analysis with BERTopic modeling
- Published in Journal of Big Data (peer-reviewed, Springer)
- International author team from India, Norway
- Key finding: novel intersections emerging between Sustainable AI and Ethical AI
- Advocates for unified approach to AI innovation promoting environmental and ethical integrity
