# AI Infrastructure Growth: Water Demand Projections to 2027

**Topic:** Data Centers - Growth
**Source:** Li, Yang, Islam & Ren, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3724499

## Insight

This Communications of the ACM paper addresses the "secret water footprint" of AI models, revealing that the explosive growth of AI infrastructure will create substantial water demand in coming years. The authors document that GPT-3 training alone evaporated approximately 700,000 liters of water, and that a typical ChatGPT conversation of 20-50 queries consumes about 500ml of water. Critically, they project that global AI demand could consume 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters of water by 2027 - more than the total annual water withdrawal of Denmark or half of the United Kingdom's annual consumption.

## Key Data

- GPT-3 training: ~700,000 liters of water evaporated
- ChatGPT inference: ~500ml water per 20-50 query conversation
- Projected 2027 AI water demand: 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters globally
- Comparison: Denmark's total annual water withdrawal < 4.2 billion cubic meters
- Water consumption varies significantly by data center location and climate
