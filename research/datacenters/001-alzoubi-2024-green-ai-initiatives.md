# Green AI Initiatives: 55 Tools Cataloged But Few Empirically Validated

**Topic:** Data Centers - Power Sources
**Source:** Alzoubi & Mishra, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2024.143070

## Insight

This comprehensive review identifies 55 "green AI" initiatives aimed at reducing the environmental impact of AI systems, but reveals that most tools remain in early development stages with limited empirical validation. The initiatives are categorized into six themes: cloud optimization, model efficiency, carbon footprinting, sustainability-focused AI development, open-source initiatives, and research community. Despite widespread adoption of generative AI, research specifically addressing its environmental impacts remains scarce, highlighting a significant gap between AI deployment scale and sustainability accountability.

## Key Data

- 55 green AI initiatives identified across industry and academia
- Six major themes: cloud optimization, model efficiency, carbon footprinting, sustainability-focused AI, open-source tools, research community
- Most tools are in early development stages
- Limited empirical validation of claimed environmental benefits
- Significant energy consumption during both model training and operation phases

## Notes

- Methodology: Comprehensive literature review plus analysis of professional websites and expert blogs
- Published in Journal of Cleaner Production (peer-reviewed)
- Authors from Middle East Technical University (Turkey) and independent
- Key gap identified: empirical validation of green AI tools is lacking
- Highlights tension between AI adoption speed and sustainability research
