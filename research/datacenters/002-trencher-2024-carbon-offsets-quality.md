# Major Companies' Demand for Low-Quality Carbon Offsets Undermines Climate Integrity

**Topic:** Data Centers - Power Sources
**Source:** Trencher, Nick, Carlson & Johnson, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51151-w

## Insight

This Nature Communications study reveals that major corporations are purchasing low-quality carbon offsets, fundamentally undermining the climate integrity of the voluntary carbon market. This directly challenges tech companies' claims of "carbon neutrality" - while they may purchase carbon offsets and renewable energy certificates to claim green credentials, the actual climate benefit of these instruments is often questionable. The gap between corporate sustainability claims and actual emissions reductions represents a form of "greenwashing" that allows data center operators to continue high-carbon operations while appearing environmentally responsible.

## Key Data

- Major companies actively purchasing low-quality carbon offsets
- Voluntary carbon market integrity compromised by offset quality issues
- Corporate "carbon neutral" claims often rely on questionable offset instruments
- Gap identified between claimed climate action and actual emissions reductions

## Notes

- Published in Nature Communications (high-impact peer-reviewed journal)
