# Red AI vs Green AI: The Tension Between Performance and Sustainability

**Topic:** Data Centers - Power Sources
**Source:** Barbierato & Gatti, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3360705

## Insight

This IEEE Access survey introduces a critical distinction between "Red AI" (resource-intensive AI prioritizing accuracy) and "Green AI" (sustainable AI prioritizing efficiency). The pervasive deployment of deep learning models has prompted concerns about ecological footprint due to exorbitant energy consumption during training and inference. Unless complex training/inference procedures and software models mitigate environmental impact, it will be necessary to decrease accuracy in production systems - inevitably conflicting with the objectives of major AI vendors. This reveals a fundamental tension in data center operations: the business incentive for maximum performance directly opposes sustainability goals.

## Key Data

- Red AI: trained using resource-intensive methods on very large datasets, prioritizes accuracy
- Green AI: uses smaller datasets, less computationally intensive training, sustainable energy resources
- Major debate emerging around balancing sustainability with AI performance
- Complex models (GPT, BERT) driving significant ecological consequences
- Environmental cost categorized by: computer architectures, data structures, and algorithms
