# CSRD and ESG Regulation: Pushing Mandatory Climate Disclosure

**Topic:** Policy - Proposed Regulation
**Source:** Oliver Yebenes, M., 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40822-023-00251-x

## Insight

The European Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) is pushing ESG indicators from voluntary disclosure to mandatory reporting requirements. This regulatory development transforms non-financial information into a key dataset for investors and stakeholders, with significant implications for corporations' commitment to carbon neutrality. Credit rating agencies are increasingly incorporating ESG assessments, transmitting the importance of sustainability indicators to financial markets.

## Proposed Requirements

- **Mandatory ESG disclosure:** Non-financial information becomes required reporting for large companies
- **Triple perspective:** Environmental, social, and governance risks must all be assessed and reported
- **External reporting integration:** Corporations must reflect their ecological footprint in external reporting
- **Investor transparency:** Enhanced disclosure to help investors understand companies' sustainability commitments
- **Carbon neutrality pathway:** Required information on companies' transition to neutral carbon economy
- **Timeline:** CSRD requirements phasing in from 2024
- **Jurisdiction:** European Union with extraterritorial impact on global companies

## Market Implications

- Credit rating agencies becoming important actors in sustainability assessments
- Financial impacts of ESG factors increasingly relevant to capital markets
- Growing demand for non-financial indicators creates standardization pressure
- Need to align financial profitability indicators with sustainability indicators

## Relevance to AI Environmental Impact

CSRD requirements will capture tech companies' data center energy consumption, renewable energy claims, and carbon emissions. The integration of ESG into financial analysis creates market pressure for genuine sustainability performance beyond voluntary pledges.
