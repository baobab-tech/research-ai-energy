# US states legislated on data-centre cost allocation in 2026; water and energy disclosure bills were mostly introduced and not enacted

**Topic:** Policy and regulation — what US state data-centre law actually passed in 2026
**Source:** Virginia General Assembly 2026 session; MultiState legislative tracking; ArentFox Schiff survey
**Type:** state legislation / legislative tracking
**URL:** https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/3/3/state-data-center-water-usage-legislation-gains-momentum
**Source note:** and https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/3/30/virginia-lawmakers-pass-15-data-center-bills-as-tax-exemption-fight-looms
**Published:** 2026-03-03 and 2026-03-30

## Finding

Where US states legislated on data centres in 2026, they legislated about who pays for electricity, not about what facilities emit or consume. Virginia passed 15 data-centre bills in its 2026 session, led by HB 1393, which directs the electric utility to petition the State Corporation Commission for rates recovering new generating-capacity costs from customers with 25 MW or more of demand. The water and energy disclosure bills introduced across at least seven states — California AB 1577 (monthly water reports to the Energy Commission), Iowa HF 2447, Michigan SB 762, Iowa HF 2261, South Carolina HB 4583 (mandatory closed-loop cooling, zero net water withdrawal), Kansas SB 400, Georgia SB 421 (banning local NDAs that conceal energy and water use) — were introduced, not enacted, as of the reporting dates. Virginia SB 417, conditioning grant funds on treated wastewater use, failed crossover and was carried to 2027.

## Methodology

Bill status from legislative trackers as of March 2026; enacted/effective dates for Texas, Oregon and Indiana from a law-firm survey current to April 2026. Virginia bill numbers and substance were cross-checked between two independent trackers. The Virginia bills passed the General Assembly in March 2026; the governor returned amendments to HB 1393 and SB 253 (Virginia's own SB 253, distinct from California's) in April 2026 which sponsors and Clean Virginia said weakened them, and the reported chaptering in May 2026 with July 2026 effect could not be confirmed against the Virginia Acts of Assembly — lis.virginia.gov serves a JavaScript application that automated retrieval cannot read.

## Limitations and conflicts

These are secondary trackers, not statute text; bill numbers are reliable, effective dates and final amended content are not. MultiState is a subscription government-relations firm serving corporate clients; ArentFox Schiff is a law firm. Sessions ran past the reporting dates, so bills listed as introduced may since have passed or died. The substantive pattern is nonetheless clear from the enacted set: every enacted 2026 instrument allocates cost or conditions tax benefit, and the water and energy disclosure measures — the ones that would generate data usable for research — were the ones that did not pass.
