# Green Public Procurement and Corporate ESG Performance

**Topic:** Policy - Green Procurement as Regulatory Mechanism
**Source:** Wang & Shen, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2025.126394

## Insight

This Journal of Environmental Management study examines green public procurement (GPP) as a policy mechanism to improve corporate ESG performance. Government purchasing power can drive sustainability when directed toward environmental criteria. This is highly relevant for AI policy: governments are major purchasers of cloud computing and AI services. Green procurement requirements for government AI contracts could create market pressure for genuine environmental performance from tech providers.

## Policy Mechanism

Green public procurement works through market mechanisms:
1. Government sets environmental criteria for purchasing
2. Companies must meet criteria to win contracts
3. This creates competitive pressure for genuine ESG improvement
4. Large government purchasing power amplifies effect
5. No direct regulation needed - works through procurement

## Relevance to AI/Tech Policy

Governments are significant purchasers of cloud computing and AI services:
- Federal agencies use AWS, Azure, Google Cloud
- AI procurement for defense, healthcare, administration
- Current contracts rarely include environmental criteria

**Policy recommendation**: Green procurement requirements for government AI/cloud contracts could:
- Require disclosure of energy consumption per service
- Set minimum renewable energy percentage
- Include water usage efficiency requirements
- Create template for private sector adoption

This represents a "soft regulation" pathway that uses market power rather than direct mandates to improve AI environmental performance.
