# Implementing CSRD Through Sustainability Balanced Scorecard Framework

**Topic:** Policy - Proposed Regulation
**Source:** Hristov, I. & Searcy, C., 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1108/md-10-2023-1995

## Insight

This paper provides a practical framework for companies to implement the European Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) through a Sustainability Balanced Scorecard (SBSC). The CSRD, effective from 2024, mandates enhanced ESG disclosure and integrates sustainability requirements with corporate governance. The framework specifies four key implementation steps: identifying material themes, initial assessment, strategic formulation and action, and reporting. This represents a shift from voluntary to mandatory sustainability reporting for large EU companies and those with significant EU operations.

## Proposed Requirements

- **Four-step implementation framework:** Material theme identification, initial assessment, strategic formulation, and sustainability reporting
- **Integration requirement:** Sustainability must be linked to corporate governance structures and performance management systems
- **Timeline:** CSRD reporting obligations began phasing in from 2024 for large public-interest entities
- **Jurisdiction:** European Union, with extraterritorial reach to non-EU companies meeting size thresholds
- **Double materiality:** Companies must assess both impact on sustainability AND sustainability impact on company

## Relevance to AI Environmental Impact

While not AI-specific, CSRD creates binding disclosure requirements that will capture data center energy consumption, water usage, and carbon emissions for large tech companies operating in the EU. The framework provides a roadmap for how these requirements can be operationalized within corporate sustainability management systems.
