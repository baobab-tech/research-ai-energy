# The Commission ran a 2026 consultation to build the measurement method the AI Act already presupposes, and to scope an AI energy label

**Topic:** Policy and regulation — the EU's attempt to define how AI energy consumption should be measured
**Source:** European Commission, DG CNECT — "Targeted consultation on measuring energy consumption and emissions of AI models and systems"
**Type:** official consultation / agency process
**URL:** https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/consultations/targeted-consultation-measuring-energy-consumption-and-emissions-ai-models-and-systems
**Published:** opened 2026-04-07; closed 2026-05-25 (questionnaire deadline extended to 2026-06-01)

## Finding

Eight months after the AI Act's GPAI energy-documentation duty became applicable, the Commission opened a targeted consultation to work out how AI energy consumption should actually be measured. The consultation supports a procured study, "Development of a study to measure and foster energy efficient and low emission artificial intelligence (AI) in the EU", with two stated deliverables: a measurement framework covering the AI lifecycle including training and inference, and the design of a "potential AI energy and emissions label". Participation was limited to companies and organisations developing, deploying or supplying components and services for general-purpose AI models.

## Methodology

The consultation is an input-gathering exercise feeding a contractor study; it produces no legal obligation. Dates and objectives are taken from the Commission's own consultation page.

## Limitations and conflicts

The respondent pool is restricted to industry — model developers, deployers, and component and service suppliers — with no stated seat for academic measurement groups, grid operators or civil society, so the resulting framework will reflect what providers say is measurable. The sequencing is the substantive point: the obligation to report energy preceded any agreed definition of what to report by at least a year, and no framework or label had been published as of September 2026. A label is described as "potential" throughout.
