# Mandatory vs Voluntary: ESG Disclosure Effects on Corporate Behavior

**Topic:** Policy - Mandatory ESG Disclosure
**Source:** Cui, Li, Xue & Zhang, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jimonfin.2025.103323

## Insight

This Journal of International Money and Finance study examines a fundamental policy question: does mandatory ESG disclosure outperform voluntary disclosure in improving corporate behavior? The research finds that mandatory disclosure requirements have measurably different effects on corporate earnings management compared to voluntary regimes. This has direct implications for AI environmental policy - voluntary sustainability pledges from tech companies may be less effective at driving genuine change than mandatory disclosure requirements.

## Policy Implications

The distinction between mandatory and voluntary disclosure is critical for AI environmental governance:

1. **Voluntary disclosure** (current state for AI energy/water): Companies choose what to report, leading to selective disclosure and potential greenwashing
2. **Mandatory disclosure** (emerging via CSRD, SEC rules): Standardized requirements enable comparison and accountability
3. **Real effects**: Mandatory regimes change actual corporate behavior, not just reporting
4. **Earnings management**: Companies under mandatory disclosure show different financial behavior patterns

## Relevance to AI Environmental Policy

Tech companies currently make voluntary sustainability pledges (carbon neutral, water positive) with self-selected metrics and timelines. This research suggests that shifting to mandatory AI environmental disclosure - with standardized energy, carbon, and water metrics per-query or per-training-run - would have materially different effects on actual environmental performance compared to the current voluntary approach.
