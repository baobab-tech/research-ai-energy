# AI Hype and Its Planetary and Social Costs

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Training (Critical Analysis)
**Source:** Markelius, Wright, Kuiper, Delille & Kuo, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-024-00461-2

## Insight

This paper critically examines the mechanisms driving AI hype and its environmental consequences, concluding that the current wave of AI hype is "historically unmatched in terms of magnitude, scale and planetary and social costs." It identifies how AI hype exerts "tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption" while also perpetuating socio-economic inequalities. The paper connects AI development directly to planetary costs, providing a critical counterpoint to optimistic narratives.

## Key Data

- Current AI hype characterized as historically unmatched in scale
- AI hype exerts "tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption"
- Identifies socio-technical mechanisms fueling AI hype: anthropomorphism, self-proclaimed "experts," geopolitical FOMO, overuse of term "AI"
- Connects AI development to redistribution of wealth and social inequalities
- Provides recommendations for developers, regulators, and the public

## Notes

- Published in AI and Ethics journal - focused on responsible AI development
- Multi-author team from various institutions
- Takes explicitly critical stance toward industry claims
- 38 citations - emerging influence in AI ethics discourse
- Methodology: Critical analysis/review, not empirical measurement
- Valuable for understanding systemic pressures driving AI expansion
