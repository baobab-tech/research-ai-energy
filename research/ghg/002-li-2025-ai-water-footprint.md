# AI's Secret Water Footprint Needs Attention

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Inference
**Source:** Li, Yang, Islam & Ren, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3724499

## Insight

This paper uncovers the often-overlooked water footprint of AI models. While most discussions of AI environmental impact focus on energy and carbon emissions, data centers also consume substantial water for cooling. The authors address this "secret" environmental cost and propose approaches to make AI less "thirsty." This highlights that per-query emissions analysis must account for water consumption alongside electricity use.

## Key Data

- AI models have a significant and largely unaddressed water footprint
- Water consumption occurs primarily through data center cooling requirements
- This represents an additional environmental dimension beyond carbon emissions
- The paper proposes methods to reduce AI water consumption

## Notes

- Published in Communications of the ACM in January 2025
- Title "Making AI Less 'Thirsty'" reflects the framing of water as an overlooked resource
- 31 citations already suggests growing interest in this dimension of AI sustainability
- Authors from UC Riverside - Shaolei Ren is a well-known researcher in sustainable computing
- Important for comprehensive GHG analysis: water treatment and pumping also have energy/carbon costs
