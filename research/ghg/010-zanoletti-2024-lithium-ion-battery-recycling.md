# Lithium-Ion Battery Recycling Critical for Sustainable Computing Infrastructure

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Lifecycle/Embodied
**Source:** Zanoletti, Carena, Ferrara & Bontempi, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/batteries10010038

## Insight

While focused on electric vehicles, this comprehensive review of lithium-ion battery recycling is directly relevant to AI infrastructure. Data centers increasingly use battery energy storage systems (BESS) for backup power and grid integration, and the embodied emissions in these batteries represent a significant lifecycle consideration. The paper emphasizes that "reuse, recycling, and repurposing of LIBs is a critical step toward achieving a sustainable battery economy" - applicable to data center infrastructure supporting AI workloads.

## Key Data

- LIB recycling essential to "minimize climate-impacting emissions"
- Hardware manufacturing of batteries creates emissions that must be recovered through recycling
- Paper covers: current recycling technologies, technological advancements, policy gaps, design strategies
- Environmental implications of LIB recycling evaluated with sustainability analysis methodologies
- Trade-offs exist between different recycling targets
- 152 citations indicates strong research interest in sustainable electronics

## Notes

- Published in Batteries (MDPI, peer-reviewed)

- Gap: Does not specifically address AI/data center battery use cases
- Methodology for sustainability analysis transferable to computing hardware
- Critical minerals in batteries (lithium, cobalt, nickel) overlap with computing hardware concerns
