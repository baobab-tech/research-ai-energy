# Understanding Environmental Impact of Generative AI Services

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Inference
**Source:** Berthelot, Caron, Jay & Lefevre, 2025
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3725984

## Insight

This paper provides an analysis of the environmental impact specifically of generative AI services (as opposed to training). Published in ACM in 2025, it represents one of the first peer-reviewed studies to systematically examine the operational (inference) environmental costs of deployed generative AI systems. The focus on "services" indicates analysis of real-world deployment scenarios rather than isolated model benchmarks.

## Key Data

- Addresses international audience with practical implications
