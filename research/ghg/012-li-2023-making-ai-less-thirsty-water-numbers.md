# GPT-3 Training: 700,000 Liters Water; ChatGPT: 500ml per 20-50 Queries

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Numbers Compilation
**Source:** Li, Yang, Islam & Ren, 2023
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2304.03271

## Key Numbers

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| GPT-3 training water | 700,000 liters | Direct evaporation in Microsoft's state-of-the-art US data centers |
| ChatGPT per-query water | 500 ml (16.9 oz) | Per 20-50 queries (conversational exchange) |
| Global AI water demand (2027) | 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters | Projected annual withdrawal |
| Comparison to Denmark | 4-6x | AI water demand vs Denmark's total annual water withdrawal |
| Comparison to UK | 0.5x | AI water demand vs half of United Kingdom's withdrawal |

## Methodology Notes

- Water consumption includes both on-site data center cooling and off-site electricity generation (power plants also use water for cooling)
- Estimates based on publicly available data from Microsoft, Google, and other providers
- Water consumption varies significantly by data center location (climate) and time of year
- The 700,000 liters for GPT-3 training assumes Microsoft's most efficient US data centers
- Per-query estimates depend on model size, complexity of query, and data center efficiency
- Authors emphasize this is a conservative estimate; actual consumption could be higher

## Critical Context

- This paper first brought AI's "secret water footprint" to public attention
- Information about water consumption had previously been kept confidential by tech companies
- Freshwater scarcity is one of the most pressing global challenges
- Paper argues AI industry must "take social responsibility" for its water footprint
- Methodology provides framework for estimating water footprint of other AI models
- 138 citations (arXiv preprint, 2023) - highly influential paper
