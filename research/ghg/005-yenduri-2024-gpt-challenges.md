# GPT Comprehensive Review: Technologies, Applications, and Challenges

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Training
**Source:** Yenduri et al., 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3389497

## Insight

This highly-cited IEEE Access paper (425 citations) provides a detailed overview of GPT models including their "working process, training procedures, enabling technologies, and impact on various applications." The review explicitly explores "potential challenges and limitations of GPT" and discusses "potential solutions and future directions." The paper represents industry-standard understanding of transformer architecture training requirements.

## Key Data

- GPT based on transformer architecture (deep neural network)
- Review covers training procedures and enabling technologies
- Documents significant popularity among researchers and industrial communities
- Identifies GPT as "one of the most widely used and effective models in natural language processing"
- Discusses challenges, limitations, and potential solutions
