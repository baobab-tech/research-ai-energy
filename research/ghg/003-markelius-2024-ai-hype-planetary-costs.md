# AI Hype Drives Unprecedented Planetary and Social Costs

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Inference
**Source:** Markelius, Wright, Kuiper, Delille & Kuo, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-024-00461-2

## Insight

This paper dissects the phenomenon of AI hype and concludes that the current wave is "historically unmatched in terms of magnitude, scale and planetary and social costs." The authors identify mechanisms fueling AI hype including anthropomorphism, proliferation of self-proclaimed "experts," geopolitical competition, private sector "fear of missing out," and misappropriation of the term "AI." They examine how AI hype exerts tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption.

## Key Data

- Current AI hype is unprecedented in magnitude and planetary costs compared to historical technology cycles
- AI development exerts "tremendous pressure on finite resources and energy consumption"
- The hype creates socio-economic injustices and perpetuation of social inequalities
- Massive redistribution of wealth associated with AI development

## Notes

- Critical perspective - examines social mechanisms driving AI adoption regardless of environmental costs
