## Key Findings from Review

- **Carbon footprint of AI is "no longer negligible"**
- AI researchers and practitioners "urged to hold themselves accountable"
- Most studies focus on:
  1. Monitoring AI model footprint
  2. Tuning hyperparameters to improve sustainability
  3. Benchmarking models for efficiency
- Mix of position papers, observational studies, and solution papers
- Studies are algorithm-agnostic, primarily studying neural networks
- Industrial parties involved but most target academic readers
- Tool provisioning (software for measuring emissions) is "scarce"

## Methodology Notes

- Systematic literature review following PRISMA guidelines
- Analyzed 98 primary studies published through 2023
- Categorized by research type, focus area, methodology
- Identified patterns and gaps in Green AI research
- Published in WIREs Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery

## Why This Matters

- 205 citations - highly influential survey paper
- Provides quantified evidence that efficiency improvements work (50%+ savings)
- Identifies that Green AI research has "reached considerable level of maturity"
- Argues time is "suitable to adopt Green AI strategies" in industrial practice
- Reveals gap: tool provisioning for measuring AI emissions is lacking
- Shows training phase dominates research attention (inference understudied)

## Research Gaps Identified

- Inference phase emissions understudied compared to training
- Limited tool support for practitioners to measure emissions
- Industrial adoption lags behind academic research
- Need to port academic results to industrial practice
