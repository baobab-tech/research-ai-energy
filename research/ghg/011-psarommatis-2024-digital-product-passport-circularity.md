# Digital Product Passport: Enabling Hardware Lifecycle Tracking for Sustainability

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Lifecycle/Embodied
**Source:** Psarommatis & May, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/su16010396

## Insight

This paper explores Digital Product Passports (DPP) as a pathway to circularity and sustainability in manufacturing, with specific applicability to electronics. DPPs provide "crucial product lifecycle information that bolsters decision-making and facilitates optimal resource management." When applied to computing hardware (servers, GPUs, storage), DPPs could enable tracking of embodied emissions throughout the hardware lifecycle, supporting more sustainable AI infrastructure through better visibility into manufacturing impacts, usage patterns, and end-of-life recycling.

## Key Data

- Provides "crucial product lifecycle information" for decision-making
