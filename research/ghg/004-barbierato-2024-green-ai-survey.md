# Green AI vs Red AI: Sustainability Trade-offs in Deep Learning

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Inference
**Source:** Barbierato & Gatti, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3360705

## Insight

This methodological survey distinguishes between "Red AI" (models trained using resource-intensive methodologies on very large datasets) and "Green AI" (models designed for similar efficiency with reduced environmental impact). The authors argue that unless complex training and inference procedures mitigate environmental impact, it will be necessary to decrease accuracy of production systems - inevitably conflicting with major AI vendors' objectives. The paper provides a comprehensive framework for understanding the trade-offs between performance and sustainability.

## Key Data

- "Red AI" prioritizes accuracy and performance; "Green AI" emphasizes efficiency and sustainability
- Energy consumption stems from: computer architectures, data structures, and algorithms
- Training AND inference processes both contribute to ecological footprint
- The survey identifies three clusters of literature: qualitative/quantitative assessments of Red AI, environmental impact of extensive DL deployment, and Green AI techniques
