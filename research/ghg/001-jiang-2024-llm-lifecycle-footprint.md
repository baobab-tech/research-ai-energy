# Life-Cycle Energy and Carbon Footprints of LLM Chatbots

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Training
**Source:** Jiang, Sonne, Li, You & You, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eng.2024.04.002

## Insight

This paper provides a crucial framework for understanding the full life-cycle carbon footprint of LLM-powered chatbots, going beyond just training to include fine-tuning, updating, hardware manufacturing (GPUs, supercomputers), data/operations management, and material recycling. The authors argue that attention must be paid to all eight main phases throughout the life cycle, not just the training phase that receives most attention. They propose a system-level solution with three strategic pathways to optimize management and mitigate carbon footprints.

## Key Data

- Identifies 8 main phases of LLM life cycle with carbon implications
- Training, fine-tuning, and updating all consume substantial electricity
- Hardware manufacturing (GPUs, supercomputers) contributes significantly
- Data center operations and material recycling also have emissions impacts
- Calls for rethinking mitigation strategies at early development stages

## Notes

- Published in Engineering journal (Chinese Academy of Engineering) - high credibility

- 57 citations as of search date indicates significant impact
