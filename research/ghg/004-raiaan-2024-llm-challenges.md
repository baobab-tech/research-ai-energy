# LLM Architectures, Applications, and Challenges Review

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Training
**Source:** Raiaan et al., 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3365742

## Insight

This comprehensive IEEE Access review (488 citations) examines LLM evolution from models with "a few million parameters" to "trillion" parameter scale "within a very short time." The paper documents how "the availability of cheap computational power and large datasets improved LLM's capabilities and raised new challenges." While primarily focused on architectures and applications, this context is essential for understanding the exponential growth in compute - and therefore energy - required for state-of-the-art models.

## Key Data

- LLM parameters grew from millions to trillions "in a very short time"
- Documents evolution from task-specific to task-independent to task-and-language-independent architectures
- Performance affected by model's depth, width, and data size
- Cheap computational power enabled larger training runs
- Training procedures and methods continue to evolve
