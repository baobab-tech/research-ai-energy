# Green AI Research Identifies AI Development Lifecycle as Critical Sustainability Factor

**Topic:** GHG Emissions - Lifecycle/Embodied
**Source:** Raman, Pattnaik, Lathabai, Kumar, Govindan & Nedungadi, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-024-00920-x

## Insight

This comprehensive thematic and topic modeling analysis of Green and Sustainable AI research identifies three major thematic clusters: (1) Responsible AI for Sustainable Development, (2) Advancements in Green AI for Energy Optimization, and (3) Big Data-Driven Computational Advances. Critically, the paper emphasizes the need for "integrating ethical and sustainability considerations into the AI development lifecycle" - recognizing that environmental impact must be addressed throughout the full development and deployment process, not just during training or inference.

## Key Data

- Three major thematic clusters identified in Green/Sustainable AI research
- Five emerging topics: Ethical Eco-Intelligence, Sustainable Neural Computing, Ethical Healthcare Intelligence, AI Learning Quest, and Cognitive AI Innovation
- Paper advocates for unified approach embedding "environmental integrity" and "ethical considerations into AI development"
- Research underscores "critical need for integrating ethical and sustainability considerations into the AI development lifecycle"
- 81 citations indicates significant influence in sustainability research

## Notes

- Published in Journal of Big Data (Springer, open access)
- Multi-institutional collaboration: India, Denmark
- Aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goals
- Emphasizes "development lifecycle" framing - supports full hardware lifecycle analysis
- Gap: Specific data on manufacturing vs operational emissions ratios not provided
- Identifies "socio-economic and environmental aspects" but hardware manufacturing impacts less detailed
