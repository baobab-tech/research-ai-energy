# Greenwashing/Bluewashing Index

Two tiers. Tier 1 is AI-specific: documented discrepancy between a named company's environmental claim and
its own underlying data (1a), plus academic work on AI sustainability claims (1b). Tier 2 is the
general greenwashing literature, retained as framing; none of it concerns technology companies or AI.
