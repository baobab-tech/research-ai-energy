# Firm-Level Greenwashing Practices: Cross-Country Incident Analysis

**Topic:** Greenwashing/Corporate Practices
**Source:** Kereszturi et al., Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental Management, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1002/csr.2945

## Insight

Using an incident-based methodology, this cross-country analysis documents patterns of firm-level greenwashing and finds that environmental policy stringency and stakeholder engagement influence greenwashing behavior. The study reveals that greenwashing incidents are widespread across industries and countries, with companies in sectors facing higher environmental scrutiny more likely to engage in misleading environmental claims to manage reputational risks.

## Key Claims vs Reality

- **Claim:** Stronger environmental policies reduce corporate greenwashing
- **Reality:** The study finds a complex relationship where stronger policies may increase incentives for greenwashing as companies seek to appear compliant without making substantive changes

- **Claim:** Stakeholder engagement pressures companies toward genuine environmental action
- **Reality:** Evidence suggests that stakeholder pressure sometimes leads to superficial greenwashing responses rather than meaningful environmental improvements
