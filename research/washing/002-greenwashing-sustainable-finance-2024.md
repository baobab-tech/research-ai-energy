# Unveiling Greenwashing in Sustainable Finance: A Systematic Review

**Topic:** Greenwashing/Financial Sector
**Source:** Frontiers in Sustainability, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3389/frsus.2024.1362051

## Insight

A comprehensive systematic review reveals that greenwashing in sustainable finance remains pervasive due to ambiguous definitions, inconsistent certifications, and lack of standardized reporting frameworks. The study identifies that corporate greenwashing exploits the gap between stated environmental commitments and actual business-as-usual practices, misleading investors and consumers who rely on sustainability claims for decision-making.

## Key Claims vs Reality

- **Claim:** ESG certifications and green labels indicate genuine environmental commitment
- **Reality:** The review finds that certifications vary widely in rigor, and companies exploit ambiguous concepts to make misleading claims without substantive environmental action

- **Claim:** Sustainable finance products align investments with environmental goals
- **Reality:** Evidence shows many "green" financial products fail to deliver measurable environmental benefits due to weak verification and greenwashing by underlying companies
