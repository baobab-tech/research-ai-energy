# Green and Sustainable AI Research: State of the Field

**Topic:** Greenwashing/AI Sustainability Research
**Source:** Raman et al., Journal of Big Data, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-024-00920-x

## Insight

A comprehensive thematic analysis of green and sustainable AI research reveals significant gaps between the field's rhetoric and practice. While the AI industry increasingly promotes sustainability narratives, the study finds that research focus remains heavily skewed toward efficiency gains that primarily reduce operational costs rather than absolute environmental impacts. The dominant framing of "green AI" often masks continued growth in energy consumption and carbon emissions.

## Key Claims vs Reality

- **Claim:** AI research is increasingly focused on sustainability and environmental impact reduction
- **Reality:** Topic modeling reveals that sustainability research in AI remains marginal compared to performance-focused work, and "green" often refers to energy efficiency rather than absolute emissions reductions

- **Claim:** Industry investments in green AI demonstrate commitment to environmental responsibility
- **Reality:** The analysis suggests that sustainability framing in AI research often serves marketing purposes while actual environmental impacts continue to grow with model scale
