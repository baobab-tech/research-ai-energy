## Scope 2, as filed

Every large operator reports two Scope 2 figures under the GHG Protocol. Headline claims rest on
the market-based figure. Physical grid emissions track the location-based figure.

| Company | Market-based | Location-based | Ratio | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Meta, 2024 | 1,358 t | 5,967,348 t | 4,394x | [020](020-meta-2025-scope2-4394x-gap.md) |
| Google, 2025 | 2,815,000 t | 15,148,700 t | 5.4x | [018](018-google-2026-market-vs-location-based-gap.md) |
| Microsoft, FY25 | 2,707,428 t | 12,030,000 t | 4.4x | [017](017-microsoft-2026-unbundled-rec-withdrawal.md) |
| Amazon, 2025 | 3,740,000 t | not published | | [022](022-amazon-2026-market-based-only-no-location-disclosure.md) |

The divergence is the arithmetic consequence of annual volumetric matching. It is evidence about
what the headline figure can and cannot describe, and it establishes intent in no case on its own.

Google's "58 Mt avoided" is defined in endnotes 142 and 144 as market-based minus location-based,
with the note that the estimate "hasn't been independently verified." Its location-based Scope 2
rose 36.9% in 2025 while its reported operational emissions fell 2.3%
([018](018-google-2026-market-vs-location-based-gap.md)).

## Counterweights recorded in this folder

**Microsoft's increase is a self-correction.** Market-based Scope 2 rose 10.4x because Microsoft
discontinued unbundled spot certificates in February 2025 and states in its own text that "our
carbon neutrality claims were supported in part by spot purchases of unbundled EACs"
([017](017-microsoft-2026-unbundled-rec-withdrawal.md)).

**Meta's procurement avoided real emissions.** WattTime's consequential recalculation puts Meta's
2023 data-centre net emissions at 770,000 t against 733 t reported, while crediting Meta's
procurement with avoiding about 7.35 Mt of 8.12 Mt induced
([021](021-watttime-2025-meta-carbon-matching.md)).

**Google backed stricter rules.** Google publicly supported tighter GHGP Scope 2 requirements
while Amazon and Apple signed a 66-party letter opposing mandatory hourly matching
([024](024-influencemap-2026-ghgp-scope2-lobbying.md)).

## Boundary and pledge accounting

Google excludes 4.37 MtCO2e, 23% of its disclosed inventory and concentrated in capital goods and
purchased goods, from the boundary it tracks net-zero against
([019](019-google-2026-ambition-boundary-exclusions.md)).

"Water positive" is reported at 78% for Google and 75% for Amazon, with replenishment pooled
across global project portfolios, with no matching to the basins depleted
([023](023-water-positive-pledge-accounting-2026.md)). Site-level water disclosure is in
[`/research/water/`](../water/).

Invalidating non-additional certificates raises the share of companies behind their climate
target from 28% to 50% across 206 firms ([025](025-bjorn-2025-stricter-scope2-rules.md)).
