# Amazon publishes no location-based Scope 2 figure at all, and applies environmental certificates against Scope 1

**Topic:** Greenwashing evidence — Scope 2 disclosure completeness; EAC use outside Scope 2
**Source:** Amazon, 2025 Sustainability Report and Carbon Methodology (July 2026)
**Type:** corporate disclosure
**URL:** https://sustainability.aboutamazon.com/2025-amazon-sustainability-report.pdf
**Published:** 2026

## Finding

Amazon's 2025 report gives a seven-year emissions series on a market-based basis only. No location-based Scope 2 figure appears anywhere in the report or in the accompanying Carbon Methodology, which states that Amazon "reports emissions according to GHGp's market-based method (MBM) in both Scope 2 and Scope 3." Amazon is the only one of the four large hyperscalers that does not publish the location-based comparison, so the divergence quantified for Microsoft, Google and Meta cannot be computed for Amazon from its own disclosures. Amazon simultaneously claims it "matched 100% of the electricity consumed by our global operations with renewable energy" for the third consecutive year while reporting market-based Scope 2 emissions of 3.74 MMTCO2e, up 34% year on year. The methodology also applies environmental attribute certificates as "carbon insets" against Scope 1 — 41,200 tCO2e of direct fuel-combustion emissions netted off via sustainable aviation fuel and renewable diesel certificates.

## Methodology

Amazon follows the GHG Protocol market-based method and applies EACs it terms "carbon insets" as abatements within Scope 1 and Scope 3, in addition to Scope 2. It states it evaluates lifecycle emission reductions, chain-of-custody documentation, fuel feedstock and third-party verification before applying each certificate. Scope 1 and Scope 3 figures received separate third-party assurance statements from Apex Companies, LLC.

Amazon's emissions model does use location-specific factors internally — eGRID sub-region factors in the US and IEA averages elsewhere — but those feed the market-based calculation rather than a published location-based series. The 2025 report changed the carbon-intensity denominator from gross merchandise sales to revenue, which breaks comparability with prior years' intensity figures.

Two facts sit in tension in Amazon's own report: 100% annual renewable matching, and market-based Scope 2 of 3.74 MMTCO2e rising 34%. Amazon does not reconcile them; the residual is consistent with matching computed over a different boundary than the one used for the Scope 2 inventory.

## Limitations and conflicts

Self-reported; the absence of a location-based series is the central disclosure gap and prevents independent computation of the accounting divergence. Applying certificates against Scope 1 is unusual: Scope 1 is direct combustion, and a certificate does not change the fuel burned, though book-and-claim accounting for aviation and road fuels has precedent under the GHG Protocol. Amazon was among 66 signatories to an April 2026 letter opposing mandatory hourly matching in the GHG Protocol's Scope 2 revision (see `research/washing/024-influencemap-2026-ghgp-scope2-lobbying.md`), and did not submit a public response to the consultation.
