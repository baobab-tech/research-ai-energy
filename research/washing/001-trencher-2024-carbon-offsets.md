# Major Companies Undermine Climate Integrity with Low-Quality Carbon Offsets

**Topic:** Greenwashing/Corporate Carbon Claims
**Source:** Trencher et al., Nature Communications, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51151-w

## Insight

Major corporations are systematically purchasing low-quality carbon offsets to meet their carbon neutrality pledges, undermining the integrity of the voluntary carbon market. The study found that large companies disproportionately demand offsets from forestry projects that have been independently assessed as having questionable additionality or permanence, allowing them to claim carbon neutrality without achieving meaningful emissions reductions.

## Key Claims vs Reality

- **Claim:** Companies claim "carbon neutral" status through verified offset purchases
- **Reality:** Research shows major purchasers systematically select cheaper, lower-quality offsets with dubious climate benefits, particularly forest-based credits that have been shown to overestimate carbon storage

- **Claim:** Voluntary carbon markets provide transparent, verified emissions reductions
- **Reality:** The demand from major corporations for low-quality offsets signals that market incentives favor cost over climate integrity
