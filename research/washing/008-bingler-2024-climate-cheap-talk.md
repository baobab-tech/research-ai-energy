# Climate Disclosure Cheap Talk: Gap Between Words and Actions

**Topic:** Greenwashing - Reporting Gaps
**Source:** Bingler et al., Journal of Banking & Finance, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbankfin.2024.107191

## Insight

Using ClimateBert CTI, a deep learning algorithm trained to detect empty climate rhetoric, researchers analyzed annual reports from MSCI World index firms and found that current climate disclosures systematically "suffer from imprecision, inaccuracy, and greenwashing." Critically, voluntary climate disclosures are associated with MORE cheap talk, not less. Only targeted climate engagement correlates with reduced empty rhetoric, exposing a fundamental gap between disclosure quantity and disclosure quality.

## Key Gaps Identified

- **Voluntary disclosure paradox:** Companies with voluntary climate disclosures use more "cheap talk" than those with mandatory reporting, suggesting self-selected disclosure enables greenwashing
- **Words vs. emissions:** Cheap talk correlates with INCREASED emissions growth, meaning companies with the most impressive-sounding reports often have the worst actual environmental performance
- **Reputation risk exposure:** Cheap talk correlates with increased negative news coverage, indicating markets are beginning to detect disclosure-performance gaps
- **Missing verification:** No systematic mechanism exists to validate whether climate claims translate to action

## Notes

- Published in Journal of Banking & Finance (85 citations)
