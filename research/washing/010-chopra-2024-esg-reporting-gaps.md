# Navigating ESG Reporting Challenges: Systemic Gaps in Sustainability Disclosure

**Topic:** Greenwashing - Reporting Gaps
**Source:** Chopra et al., Sustainability, 2024
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/su16020606

## Insight

Despite ESG reporting becoming a "global standard in financial markets," persistent concerns surround its quality and tangible impact on Sustainable Development. The study identifies a critical missing element: the "scarcity of interdisciplinary expertise across diversified fields" needed for robust reporting. Current company-centric ESG frameworks fail to capture environmental and social impacts adequately, requiring fundamental adaptation of accounting systems to move beyond self-serving disclosure formats.

## Key Gaps Identified

- **Quality vs. quantity gap:** ESG reporting volume has exploded but quality remains poor due to lack of standardized metrics
- **Company-centric bias:** Current frameworks focus on company perspective rather than actual environmental/social impact, enabling cherry-picking of favorable metrics
- **Data accuracy concerns:** Self-reported data lacks verification mechanisms and interdisciplinary validation
- **Disclosure format manipulation:** Companies can "refine disclosure formats" to present favorable narratives without changing underlying performance
- **Sustainable Development disconnect:** ESG reporting has become decoupled from actual progress toward Sustainable Development Goals
